### Market-Research-Social-Influencer

在当今的社会，网络是人们络绎不绝的话题，特别是当社交媒体开始流行与人群中。商家开始将目光投放至这类渠道，并开始尝试利用其作为营销手段，并由此产生出大部分商户将面临的核心问题：哪些因素真正驱动用户参与网红营销？

该研究旨在探索用户参与网红营销活动的驱动因素，并结合用户类型尝试为也无妨提供策略。结合过往研究，一个假设模型被建立，并对研究样本的一手数据进行回归建模，以此验证变量之间的关系。此外，结合市场营销分层模型，样本的基本信息、行为信息以及心里信息将作为阶级聚类的变量并解读成用户画像。最后将会结合回归模型的发现以及用户画像的结论对业务提出系统的建议并提出可能带来的成果。

A market analysis of social media influencer: how do influencer marketing influence consumer engagement into social media campaigns over three aspects (constructs): Consumers' willingness to engage, consumers' self-reflection on influencers, and trust developed over influencers. Besides, we also conducted a hierarchical cluster analysis to identify profiles for different segments.

This project aims to provide insights from businesses that are willing to leverage the internet for growth generation, helping businesses understanding the mechanisms and values of conducting online social influencers, interpreting how exactly do influencer marketing help boosting consumers' engagement towards online marketing campaigns, and how should businesses conduct suitable campaigns for consumers over several segments.
